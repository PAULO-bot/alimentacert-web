Frequently asked questions

Is it “AESAN approved”?

We do not use that claim. Food-handler training should not be presented as formally approved or endorsed by AESAN.

Do I pay before the exam?

No. You study and take the exam for free; payment only comes after you pass if you want the certificate.

Can I retake the exam?

Yes. Unlimited free attempts are available before certificate issuance.

Which languages are available?

Spanish, English, Russian and Arabic. Each landing page is adapted to the selected language.

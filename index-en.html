Frequently asked questions

Is it ‘approved by AESAN’?

We do not make that claim. AESAN explains that organisations providing this training do not require administrative approval or authorisation and should not present themselves as approved or endorsed by AESAN.

Do I have to pay before the exam?

No. AlimentaCert is designed so you can study and take the exam for free; payment comes only after you pass if you want to issue the certificate.

Can I retake the exam?

Yes. The design provides unlimited free attempts before the certificate is issued.

Which languages are available?

Spanish, English, Russian and Arabic. Questions and answers will appear directly in the selected language.
